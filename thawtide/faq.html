Battery & safety

How should I charge it?+
  • Use a USB cable and adapter in good condition.
  • Charge it on a hard, flat surface, away from beds, sofas, paper and anything that can burn.
  • Don’t charge it while you sleep, and don’t leave it charging unattended for long.
  • Unplug it once it’s fully charged.
  • If it’s very cold (right after being outdoors), let it reach room temperature first.
Any tips for using it?+
  • It gets hot. Start on the lowest setting and don’t hold it on the same spot of skin for a long time. Check your skin often.
  • Don’t use it while sleeping.
  • Don’t put it against bare skin inside gloves, boots or tight clothing, where heat can build up.
  • Keep keys and coins away from the display and the charging port.
Can kids use it?+

It isn’t for children. Keep it out of reach of children.

Anyone who can’t feel heat well or can’t remove it by themselves (for example, people with diabetes, poor circulation or reduced sensation) should use it only with an adult supervising. If you have a medical condition or are pregnant, ask your doctor before use.

It’s not a medical device and isn’t meant to treat any condition.

When should I stop using it?+

Right away if it swells, bulges, cracks, leaks or smells unusual; gets much hotter than usual, or hot while turned off; hisses, pops or smokes; or was dropped hard, crushed, punctured or got wet.

Unplug it if you can do so without risk, move it away from anything flammable, and don’t use or charge it again. If there’s smoke or fire, leave the area and call 911. Then contact us, and don’t mail it back.

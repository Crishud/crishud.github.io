Shipping policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the 48 contiguous United States and Washington, D.C. We do not ship internationally yet. We cannot ship to PO Boxes or APO/FPO/DPO addresses at this time.

Shipping cost

Standard shipping is free on every order. Any shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

Processing time

Orders ship from a fulfillment warehouse in the United States. Orders are usually processed within an estimated 1–3 days (Monday to Friday, excluding U.S. holidays). Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

Delivery time

Once shipped, delivery usually takes an estimated 3–8 days. Most orders arrive within 14 days of purchase. These are estimates, not guarantees: weather, carrier delays and peak season (November–January) can add time.

Tracking

When your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking can take up to 24–48 hours to show movement.

Battery shipping

Our hand warmers contain a rechargeable lithium-ion battery. They ship following carrier rules for lithium batteries, which is why some destinations are not available yet.

Address problems

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us right away at cristian.scbv@gmail.com; we can only change the address before the order ships. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to arrange reshipping, and the customer pays the reshipping cost.

Lost, late or damaged packages

If your tracking has not updated for 7 business days, or it shows delivered but you cannot find the package, email us at cristian.scbv@gmail.com with your order number. We will open a claim with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a refund.

If your package arrives damaged, see our Refund & Return Policy.

Questions

Email cristian.scbv@gmail.com.

Refund & return policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

We want you to be happy with your Thawtide hand warmer. If something is not right, here is how returns, exchanges and refunds work.

Returns

You have 30 days after your order is delivered to ask for a return.

To be eligible, the item must be in the same condition you received it: unused or only lightly tried, not damaged, with its original packaging and any accessories that came in the box. You will also need your order number or proof of purchase.

To start a return, email us at cristian.scbv@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return. If your return is accepted, we will email you the return address and instructions. Items sent back without first asking for a return will not be accepted.

Return shipping: you are responsible for the cost of return shipping, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please check your order when it arrives. If the item is damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery so we can make it right.

Do not mail back a hand warmer that is damaged, swollen, leaking, or that got unusually hot. Stop using it, keep it away from anything flammable, and send us a photo or short video. We will send you a replacement or a full refund without asking you to ship it back.

Exceptions / non-returnable items

We cannot accept returns of:

  • Items that have been dropped, opened (the casing), modified, punctured, or exposed to water or extreme heat after delivery.
  • Items damaged by not following the instructions included with the product.
  • Gift cards (if we ever sell them).

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different color is to return the item you have and, once the return is accepted, place a new order for the one you want.

Refunds

We will let you know once we receive and inspect your return (or review the photo of a defective item) and tell you whether the refund is approved. If approved, the refund goes back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take extra time to post it.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund and you still have not received it, email us at cristian.scbv@gmail.com.

Product recalls

If a safety recall ever affects a product we sold, we will contact affected customers using the email on their order and follow the remedy set with the authorities. You can always check for official recalls at cpsc.gov.

Questions

Email cristian.scbv@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

How a store gets scored

Two criteria are checked by rule: the store's public /products.json must answer, and the number of products listed there sets the catalog criterion.

The other four are judged by AI from the store's own pages (home, about, FAQ, shipping and refund policies). For each one the AI has to copy the exact line that supports it. The script then looks for that line in the page it says it came from. If the line isn't there, the criterion is dropped and counts as “No data”.

“Yes” counts the full weight, “Partly” half, and “No” or “No data” nothing. 70 and up is a fit, 45 to 69 a maybe.

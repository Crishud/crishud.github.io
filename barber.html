This prototype shows the flows from the planning documents (3-tap booking, price list already written for the owner, phone-first owner app). The shop, barbers, prices and clients are test data. The product name isn't final, so it isn't used here.

Goal from the plan

From sign-up to first booking in under 10 minutes

Research into what shop owners post online pointed to one problem: the app is easy, filling it is not. So the price list comes already written for the type of shop. The owner only fixes what is different and publishes.

Time on this setup0:00Starts when you pick a shop type.
Client page

Book in three taps

Service, time (the first free one is already picked), then name and phone to confirm. Empty sections of the page hide themselves. When the day is full, the client can join a waitlist.

  • Buttons are at least 48 px; Book is 56 px.
  • No password for clients: changes come from a text-message link.
  • The confirmation has directions, add-to-calendar, move and cancel.
Owner app · phone

Built from the project's own proposal sketch

The owner screens follow the planning team's proposal drawing (“our owner screens”): Today as the home screen, a pinned “Next” card with Call and Text, tap a free slot to book it, a new appointment in three taps by dictating, and only three tabs. Each idea in that drawing is credited to the app it came from or the flaw it fixes.

  • Try: tap a free slot, use the + button, dictate “Jordan, skin fade, at 2:00”.
  • Money stays hidden: there are no online payments in version 1.
  • Heavy settings live on the desktop panel below.

What I set out to do

Sell one product well: a peanut-shaped, rechargeable hand warmer with a page made for it. The store stays closed until I have the supplier data, a physical sample and the battery report. Until then, anything that depends on them says PENDING on the page.

Decisions I made

  • A hand warmer for the cold, picked over my first product idea.
  • The peanut shape, not the rectangle.
  • The name Thawtide, with three identity routes drawn for it.
  • No warmer gets sold without a battery-cell and circuit report. Safety first, on paper.
  • $29.99 is provisional until the sample arrives.
  • The theme ships as a zip, so the live store is never edited by hand.

Under the hood

What I used, and why.

Shopify and LiquidThe theme is built as Liquid sections.
three.jsThe warmer turns in 3D right on the product page. Its thickness is an estimate until the sample arrives.
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScriptThe mockup runs without a framework, so every section can be judged on its own.
Shipped as a zipThe live store is never edited by hand. A new zip replaces the old theme.
PENDING markersAnything that depends on supplier data is marked on the page until it’s confirmed.
AI images, labelledProduct images are AI-made for now, and the page says so.

What I set out to do

A shop signs up and takes its first real booking the same day. The client books with a name and a phone number, no account. Before deciding anything, I went through the main competitors screen by screen and read what owners and clients complain about.

Decisions I made

  • Book without an account: name and phone, and the phone is the key.
  • The app never recommends another shop to someone who didn’t ask. A shop’s clients stay its clients.
  • A full price list, visible, with no hidden fees. Fees were the top complaint in the reviews I read.
  • Big buttons: 48px minimum, 56px for “Book”.
  • Review requests go to every client, never only to the happy ones.
  • Self-service: nobody sets a shop up by hand.

How it works

Three pieces, all still planned: the owner’s app, a public booking page per shop, and the client’s phone.

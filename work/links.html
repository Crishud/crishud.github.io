links page.

A one-page shop where a creator lists the products they use and sends people to Amazon.

I measured real creator pages on Amazon, LTK and Linktree first, then built a version with no ads from other brands. It’s in Spanish, and every product and photo here is simulated.

What I set out to do

A creator gets asked the same thing over and over: what is that, and where did you get it? I wanted one link that answers it, and that the creator can change from a phone without calling anyone.

Decisions I made

  • Amazon’s rules first. The disclosure sits under the tagline and in the footer, every link carries #ad, there are no prices, and visitors always click before they leave.
  • Big photos, little text. Titles go under the photo, because you can’t guarantee contrast on photos someone else uploads.
  • No ads from other brands. That’s the reason to have your own page.

The plan, and where it stands

  1. DoneMeasured 4 real pages: two Amazon storefronts, one LTK profile and one Linktree.
  2. DoneCollections, a kit view grouped by step, a button that sends the whole kit to the cart, and an edit mode.
  3. PendingA server, so edits reach every visitor. Today they stay on the device that made them.
  4. PendingReal affiliate links.
